Test Clips

A temporary page for testing the tuner and the "listen to my guitar" checks. It records through the same microphone settings the app uses. Your clips stay on this phone until you send them.

Before you start

  • Use the guitar a beginner would have (your acoustic is ideal), tuned with your own tuner.
  • Put the phone where a student would: on a table or music stand about an arm's length away, bottom edge (the mic) toward the guitar. Unless a clip says otherwise, keep it there.
  • Quiet room, and don't talk during a clip (the clip names say what's in it). Hold the strings still when you press Record and when you stop.
  • Pluck with your thumb, the way the students are taught.
  • Anything worth knowing (a string buzzed, the dog barked, what your tuner said) goes in the note box under the clip.

Sending

Tap Send clips at the bottom. On the iPhone, choose Save to Files, then attach them in the Claude chat with the + button. A short "info" file comes along with them (which phone, mic settings, your notes).
